## What is BillingOS?

BillingOS is a developer toolkit that lets you add billing to any web app. You get pre-built React components for pricing pages, checkout, and customer portals — plus a server SDK for backend operations like feature gating and usage tracking.

**You don't need to learn Stripe.** BillingOS handles all the payment complexity behind the scenes.

## How it works (30-second version)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the SDK">
    Add `@billingos/sdk` to your React app and `@billingos/node` to your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a session token endpoint">
    Your server creates a short-lived token that authenticates your customer with BillingOS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop in components">
    Add `<PricingTable />` for plans, `<CheckoutModal />` for payments, `<CustomerPortal />` for self-service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live">
    Customers can subscribe, upgrade, downgrade, and manage their billing. You focus on your product.
  </Step>
</Steps>
