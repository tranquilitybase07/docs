## Analytics Overview

The BillingOS **Analytics** dashboard gives you a real-time view of your subscription business health. Navigate to **Analytics** in the sidebar to access all metrics.

---

## Key Metrics

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

**MRR** is the normalized monthly revenue from all active subscriptions. BillingOS calculates it as:

- Monthly subscriptions: counted at face value
- Annual subscriptions: divided by 12 to get a monthly equivalent

```
MRR = Σ (active monthly subscriptions) + Σ (active annual subscriptions / 12)
```

### Total Revenue

**Total Revenue** is the cumulative amount charged to customers across all time — including one-time purchases and all successful subscription renewals.

### Active Subscriptions

The count of subscriptions currently in `active` or `trialing` status.

---

## Revenue Trends

The **Revenue Trend** chart shows revenue over the past 30 days (or your selected period). Each data point represents the sum of successful charges on that day.

This is useful for spotting:
- Day-of-week patterns
- Campaign-driven spikes
- Churn events (drops)

---

## MRR Trend

The **MRR Trend** chart tracks how your normalized monthly revenue grows or shrinks over time. Key events reflected in the chart:

| Event | MRR Impact |
|---|---|
| New subscription | MRR increases |
| Subscription canceled | MRR decreases |
| Upgrade to higher plan | MRR increases |
| Downgrade to lower plan | MRR decreases |

---

## Active Subscriptions Trend

Shows the count of active subscribers over time. Use this to identify churn spikes or growth acceleration.

---

## How Analytics Are Calculated

BillingOS derives analytics from:

1. **Stripe webhook events** — `invoice.paid`, `subscription.canceled`, `subscription.updated` are processed in real-time.
2. **Subscription records** — the current period prices and intervals are used for MRR calculation.
3. **Charge history** — all successful Stripe charges contribute to Total Revenue.

<Note>
  Analytics reflect data from the moment your Stripe account was connected to BillingOS. Historical revenue from before the connection date will not appear.
</Note>
