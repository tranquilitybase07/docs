## Editing a Product

1. Navigate to **Products** in the sidebar.
2. Click the product you want to edit.
3. Update any fields (name, description, features, media, visibility).
4. Click **Save**.

<Note>
  Changing the **price** of an active product creates a new Stripe price and applies it to new subscriptions only. Existing subscribers keep their current price until they upgrade, downgrade, or renew.
</Note>

---

## Archiving a Product

Archiving hides a product from the public **Pricing Table** while preserving all existing subscriptions.

1. Open the product.
2. Click **Archive Product** in the top-right menu.
3. Confirm the action.

**What happens when you archive:**
- The product no longer appears in `PricingTable`.
- Existing subscribers are unaffected.
- You can still link to a direct checkout URL for this product.
- You can unarchive a product at any time to make it public again.

---

## Controlling Pricing Table Visibility

Toggle **Show in Pricing Table** on each product to curate what customers see. This is useful for:

- **Grandfathered plans** — hide old pricing from new customers while keeping active subscribers.
- **Private plans** — invite-only tiers linked directly, not shown publicly.
- **Seasonal promotions** — temporarily show or hide promotional pricing.

---

## Product Order

Products appear in the `PricingTable` in the order defined on the **Products** page. Drag and drop to reorder them.

---

## Deleting a Product

<Warning>
  Products with active subscriptions cannot be deleted. Archive them instead. Only products with zero subscribers can be permanently deleted.
</Warning>

To delete a product:
1. Ensure there are no active subscribers.
2. Open the product → **More Options → Delete Product**.
3. Confirm. This also deletes the corresponding Stripe product and prices.
