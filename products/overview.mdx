## What is a Product?

In BillingOS, a **product** represents a single plan or item that customers can purchase. Each product has a name, description, pricing model, and a list of features. Products appear automatically in your `PricingTable` component and can be purchased via a checkout session.

BillingOS keeps your product catalog in sync with Stripe — when you save a product in the dashboard, the corresponding Stripe price and product are created or updated automatically.

---

## Product Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Free** | No payment required. Customers can subscribe at no cost. |
| **One-time** | Customer pays once and gains lifetime access or a one-off item. |
| **Recurring — Monthly** | Fixed price billed each month. |
| **Recurring — Annual** | Fixed price billed once per year (often with a discount). |
| **Metered** | Usage-based pricing. You report usage; Stripe bills at the end of the period. |
| **Seat-based** | Price scales with the number of users/seats on a plan. |

---

## Product Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Displayed on the pricing card and checkout page. |
| **Description** | Short summary of what the plan includes. |
| **Price** | Amount charged per billing period (or per unit for metered/seat-based). |
| **Currency** | ISO currency code — e.g. `USD`, `EUR`. |
| **Billing Interval** | `month`, `year`, or `one_time`. |
| **Trial Days** | Optional free trial period before the first charge. |
| **Features** | Bullet-point list of included features, shown on the pricing card. |
| **Media** | Optional image or icon for the product card. |
| **Visibility** | Whether the product appears in the public pricing table. |

---

## Product Lifecycle

```
Draft → Active → Archived
```

- **Active** products are visible in the pricing table and can be purchased.
- **Archived** products remain accessible to existing subscribers but are hidden from new customers.
