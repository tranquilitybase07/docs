title: Create a Product
description: "Add a new product or plan to your BillingOS catalog from the dashboard."
---

## Step 1: Open Products

In the sidebar, go to **Products** and click **New Product** in the top-right corner.

---

## Step 2: Fill in Basic Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown on the pricing card, e.g. "Pro", "Starter", "Enterprise" |
| **Description** | 1–2 sentences describing what the plan includes |

---

## Step 3: Choose a Pricing Model

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Free">
    Select **Free** if you want to offer a no-cost tier. No Stripe payment is required at checkout, but a subscription record is still created so you can manage access programmatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One-time">
    Charge a single, non-recurring payment. Good for lifetime deals or individual add-ons.

    - Enter the **Price** and **Currency**.
    - No billing interval needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recurring — Monthly / Annual">
    Charge on a repeating schedule.

    - **Price** — amount per period
    - **Billing Interval** — `month` or `year`
    - **Trial Days** — (optional) number of days before the first charge
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Metered (Usage-based)">
    Bill customers based on reported usage. You report usage events to BillingOS/Stripe; the customer is billed at the end of each period.

    - Set a **Unit Price** (price per unit of usage)
    - Report usage from your backend via the Stripe API
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Seat-based">
    Price scales with the number of seats (users). Set a **Price per Seat** and let your customers add users — their subscription amount adjusts automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Step 4: Add Features

Click **Add Feature** to define the bullet points shown on the pricing card. Good features are short and outcome-focused:

```
✓ Unlimited API calls
✓ 5 team seats
✓ Priority support
✓ Custom domain
```

---

## Step 5: Add Media (Optional)

Upload a product image or icon. This appears in the pricing card header and the checkout modal. Recommended size: **400×200px**, PNG or SVG.

---

## Step 6: Set Visibility

Toggle **Show in Pricing Table** to control whether this product appears publicly. Hidden products can still be linked directly via a checkout URL.

---

## Step 7: Save

Click **Save Product**. BillingOS will:

1. Create or update the product in Stripe.
2. Sync the Stripe price ID back to the BillingOS database.
3. Make the product available via the `PricingTable` component immediately.

<Note>
  If Stripe is not yet connected, you'll be prompted to [connect your Stripe account](/stripe/connect) before the product can be saved.
</Note>

---

## Sharing a Direct Checkout Link

Every product has a unique checkout link you can share directly:

1. Open the product in the dashboard.
2. Click **Copy Checkout Link**.
3. Share the link — customers can purchase without ever visiting your pricing page.
